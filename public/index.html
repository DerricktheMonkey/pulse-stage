WELCOME TO YOUR STAGE

Feel the beat.

Import an audio file to generate a four-lane chart. Press D, F, J, K or tap a lane when a note reaches the glowing hit line. Keep a key or finger held for long notes. Normal charts include long notes: keep holding the same lane until the bright tail reaches the hit line and RELEASE appears. Hard charts include chords: notes with the matching warm glow must be hit together. Hit notes in their chart order; simultaneous chord notes can be hit in either order. If you skip a beat, hitting the next correctly timed note marks the skipped notes Miss so you can recover immediately. Only Perfect judgements build combo; Great, Good and Miss reset it. Finish with every note Perfect to earn S rank. Misses cost more life at higher difficulties: Easy 8, Normal 12, Hard 20, Insane 34. Accurate hits restore a little. At zero life the run ends. Enable Practice mode for unlimited play.
