WELCOME TO YOUR STAGE

Feel the beat.

Import an audio file to generate a four-lane chart. Press D, F, J, K or tap a lane when a note reaches the glowing hit line. Keep a key or finger held for long notes. Normal charts include long notes: keep holding the same lane until the bright tail reaches the hit line and RELEASE appears. Hard charts include chords: notes with the matching warm glow must be hit together. Finish with every note Perfect to earn S rank. Misses cost 12 life; accurate hits restore a little. At zero life the run ends. Enable Practice mode for unlimited play.
