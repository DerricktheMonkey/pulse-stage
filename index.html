WELCOME TO YOUR STAGE
Feel the beat.
Import an audio file to generate a four-lane chart. Press D, F, J, K or tap a lane when a note reaches the glowing hit line. Keep a key or finger held for long notes. Normal charts include long notes: keep holding the same lane until the bright tail reaches the hit line and RELEASE appears. Hard charts include chords: notes with the matching warm glow must be hit together. Hit notes in their chart order; simultaneous chord notes can be hit in either order. If you skip a beat, hitting the next correctly timed note marks the skipped notes Miss so you can recover immediately. Only Perfect judgements build combo; Great, Good and Miss reset it. Finish with every note Perfect to earn S rank. Misses cost more life at higher difficulties: Easy 8, Normal 12, Hard 20, Insane 34, Impossible 50. Accurate hits restore a little. At zero life the run ends. Enable Practice mode for unlimited play. Press Space to freeze the song and notes without opening a menu. Press Space again to resume. Press Enter to end a solo song and see results, while playing or paused.
Play requests fullscreen. Press Escape to leave fullscreen and pause. Press Escape again for the main menu, or R to restart. R also restarts directly during solo play or a practice freeze. Choose Resume to continue your run. Songs and backgrounds are stored locally in this browser when storage is available. Import a GIF or muted video for a moving background.
Song scanning estimates sound onsets, pitch, and sustained notes from the audio mix. It cannot perfectly transcribe every instrument. Edit note timing, lane, and holds to polish the result. JSON charts can be exported and reimported with the same song.

TWO PLAYERS · THREE ROUNDS
Battle Mode
Each player starts every round with 10 lives. Pick from four random Community charts each round. If only one player misses a note, they lose two lives. If both miss, both lose one life. Perfect hits restore 0.2 lives and Great hits restore 0.1, up to 10. Survive to win, or finish with higher accuracy. Most round wins takes the match after all three rounds.
