# Result grades

Completed, nonempty charts receive:

| Grade | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| SS | Every note is Perfect, including hold notes |
| S | At least 95% weighted accuracy, without meeting SS |
| A | At least 90%, below 95% |
| B | At least 80%, below 90% |
| C | At least 70%, below 80% |
| D | Below 70% |

Failed runs receive F. Early exits and empty or partially judged charts have no completion grade. Displayed accuracy is rounded, but grading uses the exact weighted counts. Perfect contributes 1, Great 0.8, Good 0.5, and Miss 0. A full combo alone does not grant SS. A missed or early-released hold also prevents SS.

Score, timing windows, charts, lives, life recovery, scroll speed, and leaderboard eligibility are unchanged. Training results can show a grade but do not submit ranked scores. Saved historical scores are retained; new completed runs use the new grading scale.

UI headings and results use functional labels instead of promotional slogans. Regression tests cover thresholds, completion guards, results rendering, root/public parity, and browser UI.
